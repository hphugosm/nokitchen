# Food bank and household walkthrough

The [seven-step guided demo](guided-demo.md) follows a donor message through actual receipt, replanning, approval and collection. The same actions are available in the ordinary workspace. Use **Recipes** to inspect appliance, tool and stock compatibility, **Compare planners** for independent fresh calculations, and **Packing lists** to trace exact lots back to household meals.
