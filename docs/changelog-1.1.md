# NoKitchen 1.1

The existing weekly planner, catalog counts, both portals and offline export are retained. The release adds five ways to inspect and demonstrate the planning decision.

| Area | Change |
| --- | --- |
| Unmet needs | Households are ordered by coverage. Open a missing slot for its family size, collection day, alternative recipes and methods, exact required and eligible quantities, shortfalls and earlier claims on stock. |
| Strategy comparison | A simple first-fit planner and NoKitchen run on the same received-stock snapshot. Results include the least-served household, households receiving zero portions, every household’s gain or loss, and remaining amounts by ingredient. Two isolated teaching scenarios are available. |
| What would help? | The planner tests a small set of individual equipment and supply changes. Previews show gains, losses, recipe changes and unresolved blockers. A separate command applies equipment or creates a local donation proposal for subsequent review and receipt. |
| Recipes and packages | Each of the 16 recipes and 20 methods was reviewed against ingredient amounts, tools, utilities, lots and packing. Potato preparation instructions were corrected. Meal and packing views now show the actual package and opener requirement per lot. |
| Guided demo | A temporary full-week store supports six user-controlled steps, including real approval and a household’s collection boxes. Back restores an entry checkpoint. Restart creates a new tour, and exiting or reloading reveals the ordinary workspace. |

Existing saved household settings, stock quantities, received offers and active reservations migrate to the version 2 scenario format. Historical plans from 1.0 are marked out of date. Their active reservations stay in place until a release or an approved replacement. The old browser storage entry remains available to version 1.0.
