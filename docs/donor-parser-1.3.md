# Local donor text extraction in 1.3

Open **Demo donations** and choose **Paste donor offer**. Enter plain text or use an example, check the model week, and select **Parse offer**. The sender and subject are optional. The browser saves the original message locally with its source, timestamp, parser version, catalog fingerprint and a new durable offer ID. The editor keeps the message, each row’s original line and character span, the phrases behind extracted fields, operator changes, and any exclusion reason. A duplicate-text warning permits separate genuine donations with identical wording.

## Supported input

Use one product and package or expiry group per line. The parser also recognizes a continued package group introduced by “and” or Czech “a” followed by a count. A continued group can inherit the preceding product when it explicitly describes another group of the same packages. Its own dates and quantities stay on its own row. The limit is 16,000 characters and 64 rows per offer.

| Input                                                                                              | Local extraction                                                                                                                                       |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `Dry rice 2 kilograms`                                                                             | Matches dry rice and reads 2,000 g. Generic “rice” needs a product choice.                                                                             |
| `3 x 0,25 kg ovesné vločky`                                                                        | Matches oats and reads 3 packages of 250 g. Packaging and storage remain available for operator confirmation.                                          |
| `Six 500 g pasta packets, best before 2027-02-01, and four 500 g packets, best before 2027-03-01.` | Records separate 3,000 g and 2,000 g groups with their individual dates.                                                                               |
| `12 pouches, 2400 g net total across all pouches`                                                  | Records the total and count. The package size and product still need evidence.                                                                         |
| `Around 10–15 kg of bread and a few boxes of rice, probably Monday.`                               | Leaves the quantities and delivery dates unresolved. The operator can match the rice, enter confirmed measurements and dates, or explain an exclusion. |

The parser uses English catalog names and registered aliases, including common Czech food names. It ignores capitalization and Czech diacritics for matching. It recognizes metric weights and volumes, piece counts, common Czech unit spellings, English package counts from one through twelve and “dozen”, and explicit multiplication with x or ×. It converts kilograms to grams and liters to milliliters when the canonical result is a positive integer. A three-digit separator that could denote thousands requires confirmation (for example, “1,000 g”). Measurements in different dimensions require an operator correction backed by the label.

Use labeled ISO dates, for example “Delivery 2026-11-23”, “Best before 2026-12-15” or “Use by 2026-11-28”. Selected Czech labels include “dodání”, “minimální trvanlivost” and “spotřebujte do”. Relative or uncertain dates need a confirmed calendar date in the editor. Availability and the final usable model day are separate explicit choices within the visible Monday–Sunday week. The demo conservatively bounds the chosen window by the delivery and any stated label date. Retaining the label type preserves its original meaning.

## Review and receipt

1. Check the product against its reviewed catalog record. Confirm usable edible, net or drained quantity, canonical unit, packaging/access, count and size for sealed packages, storage, delivery date and model window. The filled fields and source evidence can be inspected and edited. For products measured as drained solids, the operator must establish edible or drained contents. A tin’s gross weight alone leaves its usable basis unresolved. Ordinary cans also need confirmation of their opener requirement. Explicit storage fields accept expressions such as “before opening: ambient” and “after opening: fridge”.
2. Give a reason for every excluded row. Save edits, then mark the offer reviewed. At least one complete included item is required. The packaged chickpea fixture has a separate synthetic product label and bank window that the operator can explicitly confirm. That confirmation is recorded as an operator edit.
3. Use **Preview impact** against a current baseline to inspect the assumed delivery, before/after portion coverage, household variety, changed meals, exact recipe quantities and Monday/Friday box contents. A change to the saved proposal requires fresh review. Changes to the planning context require a fresh preview.
4. At delivery, correct the actual fields. A changed package count may also require updating the total. Inspect each difference, confirm the difference checkbox, then choose **Confirm received**. This command validates the actual values and creates the received lots together under the donor offer ID. Reopening or repeating the completed receipt preserves that single delivery.
5. **Recalculate** uses the actual received stock. The resulting draft’s impact can differ from the preview. Ordinary plan approval is a separate action.

The source phrase is available in each row’s **Field evidence and operator edits** disclosure. Supported alternatives outside this bounded grammar, mixed facts without a clear quantity basis, unsupported packaging terms and incomplete labels need operator review. Operators can use an exact alias or manually select a reviewed catalog product. All controls operate locally.
