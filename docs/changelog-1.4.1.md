- Households see the most recent approval after it passes current content checks. Safe stock additions preserve the existing published meals. Unapproved work stays in the food-bank view. - The bank navigation groups planning, operational work and analysis. Weekly planning emphasizes coverage and the next action. The public site identifies synthetic data and uses **Donations** for manual and sample offers.
