# NoKitchen 1.3

- Expanded the default to 50 ingredients and 40 complete recipes with 44 reviewed methods and 21 meal families. Twenty-two new seed lots exercise both collection windows. Existing saved weeks keep their physical stock and approved reservations.
- The catalog now distinguishes catalog membership, kitchen compatibility, a whole batch from eligible stock on a given day, and actual weekly allocation.
- Fresh planning considers the legacy and family-aware heuristics and a bounded fixed-coverage refinement of the leading candidate. Ordinary replanning still prioritizes covered portions, minimum household coverage and stability. **Improve variety** explicitly trades recipes at fixed slots and portions.
- Added local extraction of pasted donor messages, editable line-level evidence, proposal review, hypothetical impact, actual receipt with quantity/date differences and household-level results. The disconnected Agentboxd/Homingbox boundary obtains messages for future review.
- Replaced the guided example with a seven-step donor-to-household walkthrough, added schema 4 and preserved the frozen 1.2 regression configuration.
