# NoKitchen 1.3.1

- A donation comparison captures the active approval, or the displayed draft when the week has no approval. The versioned approved-meals-first rule recommends a valid preserving extension for an approval. The ordinary coverage-first choice remains available when it yields a different allocation.
- The receipt and approval views show the exact meal gains, removals and changes, including lot-only changes. A household’s day and collection box show the recipe, scaled quantity, package count and lot source behind each ingredient delta.
- Weekly variety now shows separate breakfast and main-meal frequencies, actual recipes, adjacent repetitions and family balance for each household.
- The existing local storage format keeps approved reservations, stock, saved offers and drafts. Completed 1.3 receipt comparisons lacking the new rule are retired. A pending 1.3 receipt captures the active approval for its first 1.3.1 calculation while retaining the original receipt record. New comparisons retain their selection across reload.
